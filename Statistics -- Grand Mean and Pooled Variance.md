When you have multiple means (e.g., averages from different experimetns or batches), you can estimate the overall population mean by calculating a Grand Mean.

$$Weighted\; Mean =\frac{(n_1\cdot \bar{x}_1)+(n_2\cdot \bar{x}_2)+\cdots+(n_k\cdot \bar{x}_k)}{n_1+n_2+\cdots+n_k}$$
Pooled variance:
$$s_p^2=\frac{\sum(n_i-1)s_i^2}{\sum(n_i-1)}$$
